# Intake of outside material (talks, courses, articles, screenshots)

STATUS: DRAFT process. Adapted from the instruction of a separate
knowledge-base project. Purpose here: stop conference or course material from silently
becoming a rule.

## Step 1 — assess before accepting (short)
- Source: who, vendor or independent, any interest in selling something (a course, a tool).
- Currency: any date, product version, model name. Old model names or retired features → mark
  "probably outdated".
- Contradictions with what is already in `rules/`, `policy/` or this register: show both, do not
  pick a winner silently.
- Screenshot without source or date: weak evidence on its own; say what is unknown.

## Step 2 — register (`knowledge/register.md`)
Title · Type · Source · Date · Status (current / probably outdated / cannot assess) ·
Category · Key points marked FACT / CLAIM (source says) / INFERENCE (ours) · Contradictions ·
Open questions.

## Step 3 — promotion
Material becomes a rule, skill step or policy clause only through a proposal to the QA Lead
that names the register entry. One source is one opinion: say so in the proposal. A claim
that needs a number goes to `decisions/verification-log.md` and stays unused until measured.
