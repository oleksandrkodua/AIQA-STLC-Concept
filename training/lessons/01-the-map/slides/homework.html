Homework · 20 minutes

Draw the map in your own words

1. Pick any 6 of the 14 terms. One sentence each, in your own words.

2. For each term write the path in our repo where it lives.

3. Check yourself: list the folder and compare with the map slide.

If a term has no place in the repo, write "none". That is a valid answer.
