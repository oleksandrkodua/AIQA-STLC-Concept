# Animation ranking (v1, 2026-10-08)

Basis: design-critique of the 29 animation boards by three criteria: hook (grabs the viewer), teaching value (QA course), risk (readability, flashing, reduced-motion, time).
NOT tested on real viewers; it is a design judgment. Reorder after the first live lesson.

## Tiers
**S, signature moment (max 1 per lesson):** Circuit (22), Term / terminal boot (23), Slots (8), Ctx (7), Domino (11), Stamp (13), Course (15)
**A, workhorses (default toolkit):** Highlight (2), Wipe (4), Check (5), Counter (3), Main / zoom map (1), Tok (9), Gauge (24)
**B, situational:** Relay (14), Light (12), Radar (17), Parts (25), Scroll (10), GridReveal (28), Parallax (29, chapter opener only), Breathe (6), Wind (16)
**C, rare / previews only:** Glitch (18), Holo (19), Orbit (20), Rain (21), Tunnel (26), Scramble (27)

## Defaults
- Play once on slide entry; loop only in gallery previews.
- Max 1 ambient + 1 meaningful per slide; ambient (Breathe, Wind, Rain, Orbit) off unless asked.
- Last frame must be readable without the motion (check with reduced-motion).
- Glitch under 1.5 s, at most 3 flashes/s. Scramble must end on readable text.
- Stagger rise (cover, lists) is the baseline, not counted as an animation.
